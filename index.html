Import customers from a CSV file

In PrintLogic, export your customer list to CSV or Excel (save as CSV), then upload it here. The first row should be the column headings, for example Customer Name, Address, Suburb, State, Postcode, Contact, Phone, Email, Account. Customers already here are updated, not duplicated.

How to read the planner

URGENTUrgent job. Set automatically when the job says URGENT, ASAP or RUSH, or by anyone from the job panel. URGENT jobs sit at the top. 2 days overduePast its due date and not yet delivered. Due todayDue today or tomorrow. HP IndigoThe machine on the job sheet. "No machine" means it was left blank. PS DeliveryThe delivery run the job is on.
Every order entered here prints a job sheet with a barcode for each job. Scanning that barcode at a station moves the job on: Prepress → the machine's queue → Printed → Completed at Carlton or Hurstville → Delivered or Collected. Every screen updates within a few seconds.
