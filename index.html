Client list

Every new order is checked against this list. Account clients go straight into production. Pay first clients are held until an admin confirms payment. Customers not on the list are flagged to check. The list is saved in production's OneDrive, not on the website.

Company name, then Account or Pay first (Non account, Cash and COD also work). An email domain is optional and helps match spelling differences. You can paste straight from Excel.

Import customers from PrintLogic

In PrintLogic, export your customer list to CSV or Excel (save as CSV), then upload it here. The first row should be the column headings, for example Customer Name, Address, Suburb, State, Postcode, Contact, Phone, Email, Account. Customers already here are updated, not duplicated.

How to read the planner

URGENTUrgent job. Set automatically when the job says URGENT, ASAP or RUSH, or by anyone from the job panel. URGENT jobs sit at the top. 2 days overduePast its due date and not yet delivered. Due todayDue today or tomorrow. HP IndigoThe machine from the PrintLogic jobsheet. "No machine" means it was left blank. PS DeliveryThe delivery run the job is on.
Every PrintLogic email to production@ moves a job along: Created → Prepress (queued on a machine) → Printed → Scanned complete → Delivered or Collected. Delivered and Collected are the only steps you click yourself.
